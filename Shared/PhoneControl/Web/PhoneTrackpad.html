Trackpad

Spread two fingers to zoom in; pinch together to zoom out.

Acceleration makes slow movements precise and quick flicks travel further, like a laptop trackpad.

On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen → Open as Web App to hide Safari’s bars. The watch’s browser controls are managed by watchOS.

  • Tap to click; two-finger tap to right-click.
  • Double-tap and hold until “Drag ready”, then move to drag. Lift to drop.
  • Two fingers scroll up/down or sideways, and keep gliding after you lift. A quick two-finger swipe sideways goes back/forward (browser, File Explorer). Pinch to zoom. Three fingers drag.
  • Four fingers left/right change desktops. Up opens all windows; down opens the desktop (Mac: app windows).
  • Swipe Apps up to show all windows (Mac: Mission Control); swipe down to close them, or again to show/hide the desktop. Swipe Apps left/right to move between desktops (Spaces on Mac). With only one desktop, swipe through recent windows instead; lift to choose. Hold still first to open the app switcher. Tap to return to the previous app.
  • Press ⌘ (bottom left) and slide to a shortcut, then release to send it; or tap ⌘ to open the shortcuts and tap one. Shortcuts: copy, paste, cut, select all, undo/redo, tabs, find, save, refresh, search, quit and screenshots (⇧⌘3 full screen, ⇧⌘4 area, ⇧⌘5 recording).
  • The bottom row of ⌘: Keyboard types on the computer with your phone keyboard, with Esc, Tab, arrows and Delete above it; Paste to sends text, formatted text, photos, PDFs or other files you copied on the phone and pastes them; Copy from brings the computer clipboard to the phone to copy, save or download.
  • Tap Media to play/pause; swipe horizontally for tracks, or up to reveal output volume; hold to open the Phone Bar MIDI controls.
