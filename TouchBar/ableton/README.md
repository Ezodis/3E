# Touch Bar + Ableton rhythm kit

[Download the complete kit](TouchBar-Ableton.zip) · [Read the setup and reuse guide](Ableton-Guide.md)

Saved October 11, 2026 from the working Controlla tap/hold setup. Extract the ZIP into your Music folder or Ableton User Library; keep its subfolders together. It does not depend on Documents/Codex.

## Included

- Max for Live tap/hold device and editable source.
- Starter Ableton set with SONG RHYTHMS routed to AI DRUMS.
- 11 section loops and the full 43-bar rhythm, also exported as standard MIDI files.
- Drum Rack and all six drum samples.
- Actual Touch Bar MIDI layouts and mappings, plus a backup-first restore helper.
- Setup, sound replacement, tempo changes, and fewer/more CC controls explained in the guide.

Your four saved momentary pads send CC1–CC4 on MIDI channel 1. Tap makes one hit; holding for about 300 ms follows that drum's incoming rhythm. Release stops new hits. Replacing drum samples keeps the rhythm; changing project BPM changes its speed. More independently controlled parts require extending the Max device as well as adding Touch Bar pads.

Requires Live 12.2.5 or newer with Max for Live and the 3£ Touch Bar app. File/reference checks passed; the newly assembled starter requires an interactive playback check in Ableton. Existing app settings and the original song project were not changed.
